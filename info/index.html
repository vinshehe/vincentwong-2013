Info


He loves the smell of book and texture of wood.

Vincent Wong (b. 1990, Indonesian) is an interdiciplinary graphic designer currently working in Singapore. He has been practicing graphic design for various entities and institutions with wide range of projects including identity, editorial, digital interface, environmental graphic, and typography.

This site serves as windows to a selection of his notable works, also a record of his current development. If by any mean you are interested in working or initiating a project together with him, don’t hesitate to drop a mail. He will be very happy to talk about it.

contact: hi@vincentwong.co / vincentwong.me@gmail.com
