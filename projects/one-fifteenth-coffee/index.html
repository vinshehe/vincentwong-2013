One Fifteenth Coffee

Located at South Jakarta, One Fifteeth Coffee (1/15) opened its door in 2012. The name itself is a coffee-to-water ratio (one gram of coffee for every fifteen milliliters of water) that renownly yields an ideal cup.

“One Fifteenth Coffee aims to bring the community the best possible coffee experience.”

1/15 started out when the Jakarta’s coffee scene becoming hips. Their passion to serve quality coffee followed by good will to share knowledge related to coffee. This quickly made them as one of the notable coffeeshop in town.
