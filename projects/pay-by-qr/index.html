Pay by QR

Pay by QR is an electronic payment method through mobile application. As clear as its name, the payment process involved scanning and snapping QR code to complete your purchase.

Since it is a quite new payment method in Indonesia, we avoid confusing identity or being too ‘techy’. Therefore, we designed a straightforward identity with clear and direct communication. The logo was a simplification of QR structure combined with a snap icon.
