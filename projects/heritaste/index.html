2014

Heritaste

Heritaste is a peranakan restaurant located on West Jakarta. Started by a young couple with the desire to deliver traditional peranakan food — especially from Malaysia and Singapore.

The restaurant was built with highly modern spirit while maintaining the traditional roots. Heritaste features a bold color scheme that was inspired by a complex of colorful peranakan houses at Koon Seng Road, Singapore.
