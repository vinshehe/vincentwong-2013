2014

Adyaguna Architecture

An identity work for a friend of mine, Richard Adyaguna who is starting up his career as an independent architect under the name Adyaguna Architecture. The identity was developed based on the word ‘Adyaguna’ itself which is composed of two words: adi (superior, huge) and guna (beneficial).
