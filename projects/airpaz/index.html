Airpaz

Airpaz is an Indonesian online flight reservation founded in 2011. At that time, the business of online ticketing is already growing. As a new player at the field, Airpaz aim to be recognized by its friendly yet bold interface. The owner wanted red for its primary color, which for me it sure is a good way to start with.

By putting those priorities in mind, the interface was designed with as little element as possible, relying on only typography, space, and color.

The web could be accessed through www.airpaz.com, though several changes had been done by their inhouse designer.
