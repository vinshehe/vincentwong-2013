Design Heroes

Back in college days, we were given assignment to pick designers whom we are fond of.

Design heroes is a small book containing profile and works of six graphic designers: Shigeo Fukuda, Jonathan Barnbrook, Michael Johnson, Louise Fili, Simon C. Page, and Moshik Nadav.

Credits: This project was a collaboration with: Artya Chamiastri, Elfira Yunus, Erwin Yovianto, and Ivanna Cerelia Suryo.
